What is DevOps?

DevOps is a culture and set of practices that combines software development and IT operations. Its goal is to shorten the software development lifecycle, improve collaboration, and deliver high-quality applications efficiently.



Core Principles of DevOps

Collaboration

Encourages developers and operations teams to work together, improving communication and productivity.

Automation

Reduces manual work by automating repetitive tasks, increasing efficiency and reliability.

Continuous Improvement

Focuses on frequent updates, monitoring, feedback, and process optimization.



Benefits of DevOps

  • Faster software delivery
  • Improved team collaboration
  • Higher deployment success rates
  • Better system reliability and stability
  • Enhanced customer satisfaction
  • Rapid response to business needs
