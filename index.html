What is DevOps?

DevOps is a modern approach that combines software development and IT operations. It promotes collaboration, automation, and continuous improvement to deliver software faster and more reliably.



Core Principles of DevOps

Collaboration

Encourages teams to work together, improving communication and productivity throughout the software lifecycle.

Automation

Automates repetitive tasks, reducing human error and increasing efficiency.

Continuous Improvement

Supports frequent feedback, monitoring, and optimization of systems and processes.



Benefits of DevOps

  • Faster software delivery
  • Improved team collaboration
  • Higher deployment success rates
  • Better reliability and stability
  • Enhanced customer satisfaction
  • Quick adaptation to business needs
